## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a backend-focused engineer and AI builder who enjoys turning complex systems into reliable, useful products. I work as an **SMTS at Salesforce**, where I focus on large-scale backend engineering, resilient APIs, and platform architecture.

Outside of work, I build open-source packages, AI experiments, and developer tools. Most of my side projects sit at the intersection of backend engineering, automation, and applied AI.

This profile is where I share shipped projects, reusable packages, and experiments that help me learn in public.

---

## 🎯 Current Focus

- Building practical AI tools for developers and everyday workflows
- Designing scalable backend systems with clear observability and operational behavior
- Publishing small, focused open-source packages that solve real problems
- Exploring product ideas that combine APIs, automation, and intelligent interfaces
